# Kasetsart ECE Department Home - Notion Example
## Before & After: From Scattered to Unified

---

## BEFORE (Without Notion)

### The Problem
- **Course materials** scattered: syllabi in email, slides in Drive, announcements in LINE/email
- **Committee meetings** notes in different people's notebooks, decisions lost
- **Supervision records** (thesis, projects): each advisor keeps personal files
- **Staff directory** outdated or doesn't exist; hard to know who handles what
- **Research projects** tracking: no central hub, no visibility across department
- **Administrative documents** (policies, forms, guides): staff searches multiple locations
- **Teaching schedules** in Google Calendar but not linked to courses
- **Onboarding new staff/students**: manual email chains with scattered links

**Result:** Time wasted searching, duplicated effort, knowledge lost when people leave, poor collaboration.

## **How to Build This**

### Phase 1 (Month 1): Core Hub
1. Create Department Home page with quick links
2. Staff Directory (simple table to start)
3. Start Meeting Notes database for next committee meeting
4. Add 1 Course Hub as a pilot (your 01204212 course)

### Phase 2 (Month 2–3): Expand
5. Migrate all teaching materials to course hubs
6. Build Research Projects database
7. Add Policies & Procedures section
8. Set up permission levels (who can view/edit what)

### Phase 3 (Month 4+): Optimize
9. Add Supervision Hub (for thesis/project tracking)
10. Create Events & Workshop management section
11. Train department secretary to maintain
12. Gather feedback and refine structure

## **Key Notion Features Used**

| Feature | Example | Benefit |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **Databases** | Course Hub, Staff Directory, Meeting Notes | Organize info into searchable, filterable records |
| **Relations** | Supervision record linked to student, to research project | Show connections (who is advising whom, what projects are involved) |
| **Templates** | Meeting agenda, grading rubric, assignment submission | Consistency and speed—create new items from templates |
| **Filters & Sorts** | Show only "upcoming deadlines" or "my courses this semester" | Personalized views for each user role |
| **Mentions** | @department.secretary in action items | Notify people of assignments |
| **Permissions** | Some pages "Editors only" (decisions), others "Viewer" (announcements) | Control who can edit sensitive docs while keeping visible |
| **Synced Calendars** | All events from databases appear on master calendar | Never miss a deadline |
| **Inline Search** | `/search` in any page | Quick lookup without leaving your workspace |

## **What This Looks Like in Practice: A Day in the Life**

**Monday morning, 9am — Administrative Staff:**
1. Open Notion home → "Announcements" section
2. See: "New admissions forms ready to distribute; new deadline is Friday"
3. Click action item → assigned to her, she marks it done
4. Click "Department Meetings" → yesterday's decision log shows she needs to update the "Leave Policy" page by Wednesday
5. 5 minutes of clear priorities; everything is connected and transparent

**Tuesday, 2pm — A Graduate Student:**
1. Open Notion → search "My Supervision"
2. Finds her record: thesis on "Computer Vision for Agriculture"
3. Sees: next meeting with advisor is Thursday 3pm (linked to advisor's calendar)
4. Sees feedback on her latest draft (attached comment on the "Drafts" file)
5. Sees milestone: "Proposal defense" due Nov 15 (on calendar)
6. All her thesis info in one searchable place

**Wednesday, 11am — A New Faculty Member:**
1. Opens Department Home (their first click on Notion)
2. Sees: "New Staff Onboarding" → step-by-step checklist
3. Clicks "Who to Contact" → staff directory shows department secretary's office hours
4. Clicks "Teaching Resources" → finds grading rubric template
5. Searches "Leave Policy" → finds it with approval workflow
6. Ready to teach on Monday instead of drowning in email

## **Notes for Your Workshop Presentation**

**Show this example on screen:**
- Open the Department Home, click into 1–2 databases (Course Hub, Staff Directory)
- Show a staff member's entry: name, role, email, office hours
- Show a course page: scrollable, organized, all materials in one place
- Show the calendar view combining all department events
- Show a simple filter in action: "Show only meetings from September"

**Emphasize:**
- "Every page and database you saw is built by *you*—we'll show you how"
- "This is one example; your department might organize differently"
- "Start small (maybe just one course hub), expand when you're ready"
- "The structure supports *your* workflow, not the other way around"

**Questions to ask the audience:**
- "Which section would help you most right now?"
- "What information do you wish was easier to find?"
- "What task takes you the longest to track down?"

---

**Last section: "Building Your Own"**

After you show this example, the hands-on practice is: "Duplicate a simple template (meeting notes or course outline), customize 2–3 fields to match your work, and share it with one colleague."

That's when they own it.
