## 📊 During the Workshop

### Before You Start
- Arrive 15 minutes early
- Welcome participants as they arrive
- Ask them to open their laptops
- Have them check email for workspace invite

### Opening (0-5 min)
- Show Slide 1 (title)
- Welcome everyone warmly
- Set expectations: "No experience necessary," "Ask questions anytime"
- Show Slide 2 (agenda)

### First 5 Slides (5-20 min)
- Present Slides 3-5 with energy
- Pause for questions
- Show Slide 6 (ECE Department Home) and click around to show the flow
- **Key point:** Make it real—"This is what your department could look like"

### Live Demo (20-35 min)
- Open Notion on projector
- Slowly work through Slide 7 instructions
- **Timing:** You talk/type, pause for people to catch up
- Circulate after demo: "Does everyone have a page created?"
- Troubleshoot account/invite issues
- Celebrate: "Nice! You just created a Notion page"

### Hands-On Practice (35-1:00 min)
- Send template links via email/chat
- Slide 9: Explain which templates are available
- Participants duplicate & customize (you set timer for 15 min)
- **Your role:** Walk around, help stuck people, celebrate progress
- Last 5 minutes: Ask for 2-3 volunteers to share their page (1-2 min each)
- Emphasize: "See? That took 15 minutes and you have a working tool"

### Wrap-Up (1:00-1:25 min)
- Slide 10: Go over workspace rules (quick)
- Slide 11: Emphasize multiple ways to get help
- Slide 12: Recap what they accomplished
- Slide 13: Final thanks
- **Distribute handouts** as people leave
- Collect feedback (scan QR code or email form)

### After Everyone Leaves
- Note which participants seemed confused (plan follow-up help)
- Collect feedback forms
- Send thank-you email with resources
- Update office hours info if needed

## 🆘 Troubleshooting During Workshop

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Someone didn't get workspace invite | Resend it; have them check spam folder |
| Page creation is failing | Refresh browser; try creating page again |
| Can't find slash menu | Make sure cursor is in text area, not sidebar |
| Template won't duplicate | Wrong template link; send correct one |
| Can't see duplicate button | Might be viewing shared read-only link; use right link |
| Internet is slow | Have offline demo ready (screenshot) |
| Projector not working | Have backup plan: show on laptop/tablet to group |
