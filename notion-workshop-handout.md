# Notion Mini-Workshop Handout
### For University Staff

---

## 1. Why Notion?

Notion is an all-in-one workspace that replaces scattered emails, shared drives, and sticky notes with a single connected hub for notes, documents, tasks, and team knowledge.

**Today's goals:**
- Understand the basic building blocks of Notion (pages and blocks)
- Create and format your own page
- Duplicate and customize a ready-made template
- Know where to go for help after today

---

## 2. Key Concepts

| Concept | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Page** | A single document/canvas — the basic unit in Notion |
| **Block** | Any piece of content on a page (text, checklist, table, image, etc.) |
| **Slash command (/)** | Type `/` to insert any block type quickly |
| **Database** | A structured collection of pages with properties, filters, and views (Kanban, calendar, table, list) |
| **Template** | A pre-built page/database you can duplicate as a starting point |
| **Workspace** | The shared environment where your team's pages and databases live |

---

## 3. Three Skill Tiers

| Tier | Skills | Who needs this |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Navigate | Sidebar, search, favorites, notifications | Everyone |
| 2. Contribute | Creating pages, basic blocks, checklists, comments, mentions | Everyone |
| 3. Organize | Databases, properties, filters, views, relations | Power users / admins |

*Today's session focuses on Tiers 1 and 2. Tier 3 is optional follow-up training for those managing shared databases.*

---

## 4. Hands-On Exercise: Build Your First Page

Follow along and try each step yourself:

1. Open the shared workspace and click **+ New Page** in the sidebar.
2. Give your page a title and pick an icon/cover (optional).
3. Type `/` and try inserting: a heading, a checklist, a callout box, and a simple table.
4. Add a to-do item and check it off.
5. Type `@` to mention a colleague or a date.
6. Share your page or add a comment on someone else's.

---

## 5. Practice with a Template

1. Go to **Templates** in the sidebar (or open the shared "Workshop Template" page).
2. Click **Duplicate** to copy it into your own space.
3. Customize it: rename it, edit a few fields, add your own task or note.
4. Explore one database view (Table, Board/Kanban, or Calendar) if the template includes one.

**Good starter templates:**
- Meeting notes / task tracker
- Staff directory or team intro page
- Event or training planning template
- Department wiki / knowledge base page

---

## 6. Quick Reference: Useful Blocks

- **Text** — plain paragraph
- **Heading 1/2/3** — section titles
- **Checklist** — to-do items with checkboxes
- **Table** — simple rows/columns
- **Callout** — highlighted note or tip box
- **Divider** — visual separator between sections
- **Toggle** — collapsible/expandable content
- **Database (inline or full page)** — structured, filterable collections

---

## 7. Workspace Etiquette (Suggested Ground Rules)

- Use consistent page titles and icons so pages are easy to find.
- Ask before deleting or heavily restructuring shared pages.
- Start with **Viewer/Commenter** access on shared pages; request edit access only when needed.
- Keep personal practice pages separate from official department pages.
- When in doubt, duplicate a page before editing it heavily.
